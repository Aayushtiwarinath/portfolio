Student Data Analyzer

Minor Project 1 · pure NumPy, no pandas

A from-scratch analytics pipeline over 60 students across 5 subjects, built entirely on NumPy array reductions — sum, mean, argsort, percentile. Computes each student's percentile rank within the class, per-subject pass rates and spread, and generates automatic topper and failure reports before visualizing the score distributions.

Customer Segmentation System

Minor Project 4 · K-Means + PCA, 900 customers

Segments customers by behavior (recency, frequency, monetary value, discount usage, category diversity) into four groups — Champions, At-Risk, Bargain Hunters, New/Low-Engagement — chosen by comparing the elbow method against silhouette score rather than trusting either alone. PCA reduces the 8 scaled features to 2 dimensions for visualization only; clustering runs on the full feature space.

04

Insurance Charge Prediction

Minor Project 5 · regression, 5 models compared

Predicts annual medical insurance charges from demographic and lifestyle attributes. The interesting part isn't the algorithm — it's the feature engineering: interaction terms (smoker_bmi, smoker_age) that capture how smoking amplifies the cost effect of age and BMI rather than just adding to it, which the EDA showed as two distinct slopes in the scatter plot.

best model: XGBoost test R² 0.90 RMSE ₹4,294

06

Customer Churn Prediction

Binary classification · IBM Telco dataset, 7,043 customers

Six models compared under real class imbalance (~27% churn) — Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, SVM, KNN — selected by ROC-AUC rather than accuracy, since accuracy is misleading when three-quarters of customers stay by default. The retention framing matters here: missing an actual churner costs more than a wasted discount, so a deployed version would tune its threshold toward recall, not raw accuracy.

best model: Logistic Regression ROC-AUC 0.843 recall-first evaluation
scikit-learnimbalanced classification
07

AI Face Image Classification

Major Project · authentic vs. AI-generated faces, 6,557 images

Classifies facial images as authentic or AI-generated — 2,790 real portraits sourced from Unsplash against 3,767 synthetic faces produced with StyleGAN3. Rather than working from raw pixels, the model learns from metadata: image quality, resolution, confidence score, and detection-difficulty labels, then is scored on precision and recall as well as accuracy, since a face-verification system that's only "usually right" isn't good enough.

6,557 images 57% fake (StyleGAN3) · 43% real 95.8% accuracy, all 3 models best F1: SVM (RBF) 0.904
